# Privacy Policy for Picyum

**Last updated:** 17 September 2026

Picyum ("the app", "we", "us") is a food logging app that reads a photo of a meal
and estimates its nutrition. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who
it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Picyum is operated by Jin, who is the data controller for the
information described here. If you have any question about this policy or about
your data, contact us at **picyumai@gmail.com**.

## 1. Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work. There is no advertising network, no
tracking across other apps or websites, and no sale of personal information.

### Account information

When you create an account we receive your **email address** and, if you sign in
with Google, the basic profile information Google returns for sign-in. We never
receive your Google password.

### Health and fitness information

During onboarding, and whenever you change your goals, the app stores the
information it needs to calculate your targets:

- sex and date of birth
- height and body weight, including later weight entries over time
- activity level, target weight and target rate of change
- your daily calorie and nutrient goals

### Meal and activity records

- meals you save, including the food names, gram amounts and nutrition values
- the time each meal was eaten
- exercise entries you add
- dishes you choose to save for reuse

### Meal photos

A photo you take or select is sent to our AI provider so the food in it can be
identified. **A photo is only stored on our servers if you go on to save the
meal.** If you discard a scan, the photo is not kept.

Stored photos are held in a private storage area. They are not public, and they
are only readable through short-lived links issued to your own signed-in account.

### Usage records

For each AI request we record the time, which feature was used, which model and
prompt version answered, how long it took, and how many tokens it consumed. This
is used to enforce usage limits and to monitor cost and quality. **It does not
include the food in your photo or the contents of your meal.**

### Technical information

Like any online service, our infrastructure records technical details whenever the
app connects to it. This includes your **IP address**, the kind of device and app
version making the request, and the time it was made. Our hosting provider holds
these in server and authentication logs. We use them to keep accounts secure, to
investigate abuse, and to diagnose faults.

### Support correspondence

If you email us, we keep your message and our reply so that we can deal with your
request and anything that follows from it.

### Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your precise location, contacts, browsing history,
advertising identifiers, or any payment card details.

## 2. How we use your information

- to identify food in your photos and estimate nutrition
- to calculate your daily targets and show your progress over time
- to keep your records available on your account
- to apply fair-use limits to AI requests and to control our costs
- to manage your subscription and restore purchases
- to send reminder notifications, if you turn them on
- to diagnose errors and improve accuracy

We do not use your data to train AI models, and we do not permit our AI provider
to do so either.

### Our legal basis for using it

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, data protection law
requires us to have a legal basis for each use. Ours are:

- **Performance of our contract with you** — running your account, identifying
  food in your photos, calculating your targets and keeping your records. We
  cannot provide the app without this.
- **Our legitimate interests** — keeping the service secure, preventing abuse,
  applying fair-use limits to control our costs, and diagnosing errors. We use no
  more than these purposes need.
- **Your consent** — reminder notifications, which stay off unless you turn them
  on. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app.
- **Compliance with a legal obligation** — where the law requires us to keep or
  disclose information.

## 3. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers. Each receives only what it needs.

| Provider | What it receives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **OpenAI** | Your meal photo, and the ingredient list you confirm | To identify food and estimate nutrition |
| **Supabase** | All account, profile, meal and photo data | Database, authentication and file storage |
| **RevenueCat** | A pseudonymous user identifier and purchase receipts | To manage subscriptions and restore purchases |
| **Google** | Your sign-in request | Only if you choose to sign in with Google |
| **Apple / Google Play** | Your payment | The app store processes payment; we never see card details |

Photos and ingredient lists sent to OpenAI are processed to return a result and
are not used to train their models. OpenAI may hold them for a short period to
check for misuse of its service, and then deletes them. Details are in the
[OpenAI API data usage policy](https://openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies).

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or where it
is necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.

## 4. Where your data is held

Your data is stored on servers operated by Supabase, and AI requests are
processed by OpenAI. Both may process data in countries other than your own,
including the United States. When we transfer data out of the United Kingdom or
the European Economic Area, we rely on the safeguards those providers put in
place, such as standard contractual clauses.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your
account, your profile, meals, meal photos, exercise entries, weight history and
saved dishes are deleted.

Anonymous usage counters that contain no food or personal content may be retained
for cost accounting. Server and authentication logs, including IP addresses, are
kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting and are then discarded.
Backups may persist for a short period before they are overwritten in the normal
course of operation.

## 6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data from inside the app, at
**Settings → Delete account**. This cannot be undone.

If you would rather we did it for you, or if you cannot access the app, email
**picyumai@gmail.com** from the address on your account and we will delete it.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, because subscriptions are
billed by the app store rather than by us. Cancel it in your Apple or Google
account before deleting, or it will continue to renew.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you
- correct information that is inaccurate
- delete your information
- receive a copy of your information in a portable form
- object to or restrict how we use your information
- withdraw consent you have previously given

Most of these you can exercise directly in the app. For anything else, email
**picyumai@gmail.com** and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the
right to complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context
behavioural advertising.

## 8. Children

Picyum is not intended for children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it,
or at least 16 if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. We
do not knowingly collect information from children below that age. If you believe
a child has given us their information, email **picyumai@gmail.com** and we will
delete it.

## 9. Security

Access to your records is enforced at the database level, so one account cannot
read another's data. Photos are stored in a private area reachable only through
short-lived links issued to your own account. Data is encrypted in transit.

No service can promise perfect security, but we work to protect your information
and to limit what we collect in the first place.

## 10. Nutrition estimates are not medical advice

Nutrition values in Picyum are AI estimates and can be wrong. They are for general
information only and are not medical or dietary advice. Do not rely on them to
manage a medical condition. Speak to a qualified professional before making
significant changes to your diet.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If the
change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
